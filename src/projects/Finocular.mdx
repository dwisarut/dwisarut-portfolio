This is the expense tracker, but with the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology. The different is that, not only the tracker can visualize the cash flow,
but it also summarize the revenue and expense with line graph, resulting in the more in-depth analysis of my own spending. Furthermore, I utilize the OCR to easily collect
the receipt for faster input time.

### The problem

---

The problems that I try to solve with Finocular:

- Break down my spending habits.
- Visualize my cash flow.
- Trouble keeping records of my spending.

### Action

---

For each points, I implement the following features:

- **Break down my spending habits.**
  1. Categorizing my spending into multiple categories.
  2. Implement line chart and pie chart for presentation.
- **Visualize my cash flow.**
  1. Use line chart to visualize revenue and expense.
  2. Filter recent transaction and display it as card.
  3. Show the total net gain or loss.
- **Trouble keeping records of my spending.**
  1. Implement OCR technology to extract the number.
  2. Manual input filled with OCR for user data validation.
