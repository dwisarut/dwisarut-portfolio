This is the commissioned project from synthetic flower decoration company (let's called them Flower Corp.), which can fetch pictures from Google Drive via Breadth-First Search Algorithm and Google API.
However, I'm not responsible for the search algorithm implementation, but the LINE bot's API and LINE webhook API integration.

### The problem

---

The problems that our customer presented are mainly:

1. Difficulty tracking the prop's progress.
2. Communication between Flower Corp. and their customer, or their internal communication.
3. They used to have their own functioning bot, but their bot's service is discontinue.

As you can see, this is their requirement that we need to address, but we also encounter multiple problems while we developing the bot.
